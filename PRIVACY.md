# PastePilot — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 29, 2026_

PastePilot helps you save, organize, and quickly insert reusable text snippets into websites to reduce repetitive typing.

**Short version: PastePilot has no account system, servers, analytics, or automatic network transmission. Your snippets and settings are stored locally in your browser and never leave your device unless you explicitly export or share them.**

## What data the extension stores

Everything below is saved only in your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) and never leaves your device:

- Saved text snippets and folders
- Website-specific snippet assignments
- User preferences and extension settings
- Keyboard shortcut preferences
- Recently used snippets and other locally generated metadata

## What we don't do

- We don't automatically transmit, sell, or share your data.
- We don't have accounts, servers, analytics, or trackers.
- We don't collect browsing history.
- We don't read or store the content of webpages beyond what is necessary to insert a snippet after you explicitly choose to do so.
- We don't upload your snippets or personal information to external services.

## Permissions and why each one exists

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| `storage` | Saves your snippets, folders, preferences, and settings locally in your browser. |
| `activeTab` | Allows PastePilot to interact only with the currently active webpage after you activate the extension. |
| `scripting` | Injects the local snippet insertion script into the active page so the selected snippet can be pasted into supported input fields. |

## Data removal

- Delete individual snippets or folders from the extension at any time.
- Clear all saved data using the extension's reset or delete options.
- Uninstalling the extension removes its locally stored data from your browser.

## Third parties

None. PastePilot does not operate servers or automatically send user data over the network. Your snippets remain stored locally in your browser and are never shared unless you explicitly copy, export, or paste them into a website yourself.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the extension evolves. Any changes will be published in this document.
