Welcome to PastePilot

Fill the text you type every day — formatted addresses, job application answers, support replies — directly into the web page you're on.

  1. Create a snippet with the + New snippet button.
  2. Assign it to sites (optional) so it appears first on the pages that need it.
  3. Click the PastePilot icon on any page — or press Ctrl+Shift+S — and pick a snippet to fill it in.
Everything stays in your browser. No accounts, no servers, no tracking.
My details — save once, then hit ⚡ Fill form in the popup on any page. PastePilot matches these to form labels like "Full name", "Email", "GitHub", "LinkedIn". Everything stays in your browser.
